Our story

Our story started with a book.

We've always had a genuine passion for real estate — not just as an investment, but as one of the more creative spaces out there: a market where a good decision quietly compounds for years, and a bad one does too, just as quietly, until it doesn't.

That passion came with a frustration. We kept watching people around us — friends, family, people in our own network — make the same avoidable mistakes, often on the advice of agents whose incentives had very little to do with the buyer's actual outcome. The same overpriced units sold on "guaranteed returns." The same excitement at signing, the same regret a year or two later once the numbers didn't hold up. We wanted to do something about it, so we wrote a book explaining how to actually understand the market and how to choose the right property — the thinking we wished the people around us had access to before they signed anything.

Writing it inspired us to go the extra mile: to build the tools that investors, and property or investment managers, could use to run their own analysis — or verify a broker's promises before committing to anything. The frameworks and calculators now on this site are that extra mile.

The Scarcity Principle

The book is about understanding the UAE real estate market — the investment strategies that actually work, and how to choose a good unit. Scarcity runs through almost every part of that thinking, since it's one of the strongest forces behind which properties hold and grow their value, but the book is broader than any one idea: it's a practical guide to the market itself. The Ratings library, the Feasibility Study, and every tool on this site build directly on that same thinking.
